Roles and publication policy

Level 1 · Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson

May manage all editorial content, approve and publish, archive, review action history, and change institutional biographies.

Level 2 · Secretary

May draft official updates, review submissions, request corrections and send approved drafts for Leadership sign-off.

Level 3 · Social Media Team

May create and edit its own news, gallery, programme, announcement and resource drafts, then submit to the Secretary.

Publication sequence

Media draft → Secretary review → Leadership approval → public website

Secretariat-created content goes directly to Leadership review after submission. Edits to published content create a new draft revision, leaving the published version unchanged until approval.

Permissions are enforced by the server. Users without an assigned editorial role cannot access submissions. No website visitor can grant themselves a role.
