THE ESSENTIAL VALUES HANDBOOK

What we value
shapes how we live.

Eleven core values, explored through youth-led conversations and practical learning.

A HANDBOOK BY AND FOR YOUNG PEOPLE

More than words on a page.

Developed through a participatory process involving young people and education stakeholders, the Essential Values for Ghanaian Youth Handbook supports learners to reflect, discuss and practise values in everyday life.

Its 22-session programme is delivered through student-led Values Learning Communities. Peer guides facilitate discussions alongside teachers’ professional learning sessions, with school counselling coordinators providing oversight.

OUR SHARED VALUES

Eleven principles. Everyday practice.

01

Honesty

Tell the truth and build trust in everyday life.

02

Integrity

Do the right thing, even when no one is watching.

03

Diversity

Respect differences in identity, experience and ideas.

04

Equity

Recognise different needs and support fair opportunity.

05

Self-directed learning

Take ownership of learning, improvement and growth.

06

Confidence

Find the courage to contribute and ask questions.

07

Self-discipline

Practise self-control, consistency and accountability.

08

Adaptability

Respond constructively to change and uncertainty.

09

Resourcefulness

Use available skills and opportunities to solve problems.

10

Leadership

Influence others through service and example.

11

Responsible citizenship

Act for the common good of communities and country.
