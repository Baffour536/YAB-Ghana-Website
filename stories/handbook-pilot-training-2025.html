YAB supports Handbook pilot training across Ghana

YAB GhanaJanuary–March 20253 min read

From headteacher orientation to regional cluster training, YAB helped schools prepare for the Essential Values Handbook pilot in 2025.

The Youth Advisory Board supported the early preparation and piloting of the Essential Values for Ghanaian Youth Handbook in 2025, helping strengthen the role of young people in the practical rollout of values education.

On 22 and 23 January 2025, YAB participated in a Ghana Education Service engagement with headteachers from 98 selected pilot institutions at the Noda Hotel in Kumasi. The event introduced school leaders to the Handbook and sought their support for implementation.

YAB then contributed to a Training of Trainers workshop in Koforidua from 9 to 15 February 2025, followed by cluster-based training in northern locations from 23 February to 1 March and southern centres from 3 to 8 March.

The workshops brought together school leaders, guidance and counselling coordinators, education personnel and implementing partners. Participants practised model sessions, discussed ways of selecting peer guides and identified needs for accessible materials, practical facilitation and continued parent involvement.

These activities formed part of the preparation for Values Learning Communities, through which learners explore and apply the values in facilitated school sessions.

Source note: YAB Periodic Report, January to April 2025, circulated by the Chairperson in May 2025. This story summarises completed engagements and does not reproduce internal personal data or photographs of students.
