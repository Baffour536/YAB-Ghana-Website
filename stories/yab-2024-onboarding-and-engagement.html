YAB lays foundations for youth voice in 2024

YAB GhanaFebruary to December 20243 min read

In its first year, the Youth Advisory Board established governance processes and began developing structured routes for Ghanaian young people to contribute to secondary education reform.

The Youth Advisory Board (YAB) began its formal orientation programme in February 2024 under the Elevating Youth Voices initiative. Board correspondence dated 13 February invited members to a six-day virtual onboarding programme commencing on 15 February, covering operational arrangements, expectations and participating education-sector organisations.

During the year, the Board continued to develop its operational practices and youth engagement approach. A July 2024 quarterly meeting notice requested Board members to review proposed youth engagement strategy documents and operational guidelines as part of ongoing governance discussions.

In October 2024, committee appointments were communicated to Board members. The Chairperson also submitted a proposed youth and student engagement plan for the Elevating Youth Voices programme, focused on stronger student participation in curriculum reform and the development of the national values resource.

By November, notices had been circulated for a year-end Board meeting scheduled for 1 and 2 December 2024 in Accra. The correspondence demonstrates the Board’s commitment to coordinating representation, committee work and education-policy engagement in its founding year.

These early steps laid the groundwork for YAB’s involvement in the 2025 pilot of the Essential Values for Ghanaian Youth Handbook and its expanding role in the following years.

Source note: Board onboarding notice (13 February 2024), quarterly meeting notice (6 July 2024), committee appointments (2 October 2024), engagement-plan submission (22 October 2024) and December meeting notices. Meeting invitations establish planned events, not independently verified meeting outcomes.
