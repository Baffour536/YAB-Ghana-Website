YAB joins discussion on sustaining values learning

YAB Ghana20–21 October 20263 min read

At T-TEL’s fifteenth LiT planning meeting, YAB contributed to discussions on supporting learners beyond the Handbook’s current 22-session cycle.

The Youth Advisory Board participated in the fifteenth Leaders in Teaching (LiT) Quarterly Planning Meeting organised by Transforming Teaching, Education and Learning (T-TEL) on 20 and 21 October 2026 in East Legon, Accra.

Evans Junior Owu represented the Board and presented progress associated with the Essential Values for Ghanaian Youth Handbook and Values Learning Communities (VLCs). The discussions considered how to sustain values education once learners complete the Handbook’s current 22 sessions.

Participants encouraged YAB to work with T-TEL, Honour Ghana, the National Union of Ghana Students (NUGS) and other stakeholders to develop a practical, properly budgeted approach for continued values learning. The recommendation was part of a discussion; a follow-on programme has not yet been announced as implemented.

The Board also described preparations for Youth Focal Groups, designed to make it easier for students to share direct feedback on the Handbook. Twelve prospective pilot schools have been identified, but their onboarding remains subject to formal Ghana Education Service authorisation.

The meeting reinforced a central principle of YAB’s work: youth participation should inform both the content of education reforms and the long-term practical arrangements for implementing them.

Source note: Prepared from the YAB delegate’s engagement report submitted on 30 October 2026. Numerical monitoring figures in that report are not reproduced here pending independent publication checks.
