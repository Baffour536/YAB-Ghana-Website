Building practical pathways with GES and CENDLOS

YAB Ghana•June to September 2026•3 min read

Follow-up engagements focus on school-based youth feedback and responsible digital content.

YAB continued discussions with the Ghana Education Service on the proposed formation of Youth Focal Groups and with CENDLOS on collaboration for youth engagement and digital content.

An earlier engagement with GES Guidance and Counselling in June considered ways to strengthen Values Learning Communities, peer guide support and feedback from participating schools.

The Board has identified prospective pilot schools. School-level activation depends on formal authorisation and communication from GES, and the YAB continues to pursue the required arrangements.

Discussions with CENDLOS explored possible collaboration on youth-facing educational content. Specific programmes and funding remain subject to further agreement. These partnerships are central to translating youth feedback into better support for schools.

Our work continues.
