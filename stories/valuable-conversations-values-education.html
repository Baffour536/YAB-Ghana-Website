Valuable Conversations launches with a focus on values education

YAB Ghana•14 August 2026•3 min read

An interactive webinar invited young people to move from learning about values to championing them in their communities.

The Youth Advisory Board held the inaugural Valuable Conversations webinar on 14 August 2026, exploring “The Role of Young People in Championing Values Education in Ghana”.

The session brought together students, partner institutions and invited organisations. Director Gifty Bremansu of the Ghana Education Service Guidance and Counselling Unit delivered the keynote address, with contributions from Honour Ghana, the National Union of Ghana Students and regional student representative councils.

Participants highlighted the value of the panel discussion, keynote, interactive question-and-answer session and closing commitments. Young people pledged to model positive values, encourage peers to use lessons from the Handbook and support practical values-based activities in their schools.

Suggestions for future conversations included youth entrepreneurship, women’s leadership, digital responsibility, student–teacher relationships, inclusion and unity among students. The Board is using this feedback to shape future interactive sessions.

Our work continues.
