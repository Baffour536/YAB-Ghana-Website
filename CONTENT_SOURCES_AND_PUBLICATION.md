# Youth Advisory Board Ghana | Editorial provenance and publication policy

Editorial source review: 31 October 2026. This is a *public-safe* editorial summary. The full research register is in a separate private archive and must not be committed to public GitHub.

## Evidence used
- Founding-year Board notices and committee/engagement correspondence (February–December 2024).
- YAB Periodic Report, January–April 2025 (distributed 16 May 2025).
- Southern Belt Cluster Training Centre Reports, March 2025, with the shared GES Cluster Training Picture Album.
- YAB Quarterly Report, April–June 2026; YAB July–September 2026 activity progress report.
- YAB delegate report on the fifteenth T-TEL LiT Quarterly Planning Meeting, 20–21 October 2026.
- YAB concept notes on student voice and Youth Focal Groups; education-sector courtesy-visit reports.
- July 2026 professional biographies and portraits provided by Board members specifically for the website.
- YAB social-media content calendar and the 2026 YAB Cluster Trainings Photo Bank.

## Publication constraints
- Planned activities and requested authorisations are not represented as completed initiatives.
- The 12 prospective Youth Focal Group pilot schools had been identified, but formal GES authorisation remained pending as of 31 October 2026.
- Discussion of extending values learning beyond the existing 22 sessions was a recommendation, not an adopted or funded programme.
- Statistics describing VLC reach vary across internal reporting dates; any public count must carry its precise date and reporting scope.
- Personal contact lists, banking details, internal reconciliations, private governance files, donor negotiations, draft proposals, learner records and unapproved full reports are excluded from this publication.
- Selected historical training photographs depict adult educators. Continue applying consent, credit and child safeguarding checks before adding other photographs.

For a research-source index and photo folder inventory, consult the separately supplied private editorial dossier. Do not copy that dossier to this public website.
