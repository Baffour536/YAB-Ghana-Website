Protecting voices.

How this website handles communication, photographs and youth participation.

About this notice

This notice explains how the Youth Advisory Board Ghana website handles information submitted by visitors and the material published on our pages.

Information you choose to share

If you use the contact form, we ask for your name, email address, topic and message, solely to help YAB respond to the enquiry. Do not submit student records, health information, financial details, identification numbers or other sensitive data. Messages sent through the form are intended for review by authorised website administrators.

Hosting and contact form processing

The site is designed for hosting on Netlify. Netlify may process technical request data and submitted messages as part of its hosting and form services. YAB will manage access to messages and review stored enquiries in accordance with its approved data management procedures.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally load advertising trackers, third-party analytics libraries or embedded social media widgets. Opening an external social media link means the destination platform’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

Images, consent and young people

Member photographs and event images are drawn from Board-provided materials. The Board should obtain and retain appropriate permission before publishing personal photographs, particularly where students under 18 appear. Requests to correct or remove identifiable content should be directed through the contact page or official channels.

Contact and safeguarding

This website does not accept confidential safeguarding complaints or emergency reports. Where there is an immediate safety concern, use the relevant school safeguarding process or local emergency services rather than a public website form.

Changes

This notice was last updated in October 2026. It may be revised as the website and its data-handling procedures develop.
