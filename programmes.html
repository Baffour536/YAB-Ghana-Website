OUR PROGRAMMES

Ideas that move
into action.

YAB’s programmes connect youth participation, values education, school engagement and education policy.

01 / PROGRAMME

Youth Focal Groups

A feedback loop from students to policy.

Youth Focal Groups are planned as school-linked spaces in which students and peer guides can share their experiences of the Values Handbook and wider school reform. YAB’s Q2 report recorded the selection of 12 prospective pilot schools. Formal school onboarding depends on GES authorisation.

Why it mattersEducation initiatives work better when learners can explain what is happening in their own schools.
02 / PROGRAMME

Valuable Conversations

A space to listen, learn and lead.

Launched on 14 August 2026, this interactive webinar series brings young people, educators and partners into discussions on values and the choices young people face. The inaugural session focused on young people championing values education in Ghana.

What comes nextFuture sessions will continue to prioritise practical commitments, dialogue and student-led action.
03 / PROGRAMME

Policy Voice & Partnerships

Representing youth where decisions are made.

YAB contributes youth perspectives to education reform conversations through collaborations with the Ministry of Education, GES, T-TEL, CENDLOS, NUGS and other stakeholders. Board members also took part in the July 2026 national school discipline conference.

ApproachConnect lived student experience to policy dialogue and document practical follow-up actions.
04 / PROGRAMME

Digital Voice & Storytelling

Making our work visible and accountable.

YAB’s structured digital communications plan has eight editorial pillars, including Meet the YAB, Values in Action, Student Spotlight and Voices from the Community. Content is designed to share the Board’s work and amplify responsible youth perspectives.

Safeguarding mattersStudent stories and photographs should only be published with appropriate consent, supervision and review.
